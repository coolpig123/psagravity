Scores are vs FEDS, not the official area. FEDS (VIIRS-modeled perimeters) under-maps the official WFIGS perimeter (Bug 37.8 %, Biscar 11.2 % of official), so a predicted cell outside FEDS is not necessarily wrong.

The model holds the start-hour weather for the whole run (spread_model/simulate.py); see hindcast/SUGGESTIONS.md. Stale targets (FEDS had no new data) are drawn but not scored. Colwash is left out (FEDS IoU vs WFIGS 2.9 %).
